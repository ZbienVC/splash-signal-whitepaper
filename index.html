01 | Executive Summary

Solana moves faster than anyone can watch by hand. New tokens launch by the minute. Developer wallets sell before most people know a project exists. Smart money enters, sizes up, and starts taking profit while everyone else is still reading the chart.

Splash Signal reads the chain for you. It pulls live on-chain and market data, runs it through a set of purpose-built scoring engines, and surfaces the moves that actually matter as plain ENTRY, EXIT, and WATCH signals. No magic, no chatbot telling you to do your own research.

The thesis is simple. By the time a token is trending on X, the smart money has usually already moved. We give you visibility into those moves sooner. Not because we have secret data, everything on Solana is public, but because we can read, score, and act on that data faster than a person staring at a screen.

02 | The Problem on Solana

Solana is its own kind of market. Thousands of transactions per second. Tokens that run from launch to a fifty million dollar cap and back to zero in the time it takes to make coffee. In that world, do your own research is a nice idea that falls apart in practice.

What traders are up against:

  • Hundreds of new tokens every day, most of them noise and many of them scams
  • Dev wallets that rug within hours, sometimes minutes, of launch
  • Sniper bots that buy blocks ahead of everyone else
  • Wallet clusters that accumulate quietly before any visible momentum
  • Narratives that pour capital into a sector overnight and drain it just as fast

The data exists. Processing it is the problem. Every transaction, every wallet move, every liquidity event is already public. The edge is not access. It is reading and acting on what is there, faster and more clearly than the next trader.

Most tools solve one slice of this. A price screener here, a rug checker there, a bot that pings you when a wallet moves. What has been missing is one layer that ties on-chain data, wallet behavior, market signals, and narratives into a single picture. That layer is what we built.

Where the data comes from

  • Helius RPC: direct Solana reads for transactions, balances, and network health. If it is not on-chain, we do not act on it.
  • Pump Portal stream: a live connection to Pump.fun, so new launches and trades arrive as they happen rather than on a delay.
  • DexScreener: price, volume, and liquidity across Solana pairs.
  • RugCheck: mint and freeze authority status, LP lock state, and holder concentration flags.
  • CoinGecko and GDELT: broader market context and news velocity that feed the narrative engine.

What the engines produce

Processed data surfaces as three signal types that drive the live feed:

  • ▲ ENTRY: a high-confidence opportunity, usually when smart wallet activity lines up with early momentum.
  • ▼ EXIT: a risk-weighted warning, such as pulled liquidity, dev wallet movement, or coordinated selling.
  • ◆ WATCH: a token in transition, flagged for attention without a firm call yet.

05 | Core Modules

Here is what you can actually do in the app right now.

Discovery and Radar

The front door. The OG Scanner takes a ticker or contract and tells you which token is the real one, so you do not buy a copycat. Migrations tracks Pump.fun bonding curves close to graduating. Runners and Rug Radar surface tokens gaining steam and tokens showing rug-shaped behavior.

Token X-Ray

The deep dive. Paste any mint and the platform runs an eight stage forensic pass: it reads metadata, scores liquidity health, maps holder concentration, looks for wallet clusters that share funding, studies the trade timeline, then rolls it all into a single risk score and a plain verdict of SAFE, CAUTION, HIGH RISK, or CRITICAL.

KillZone

A position risk simulator, not a trade button. You enter a token and a size, and KillZone models what that position really costs once entry slippage, exit slippage, and sell pressure are priced in. A theory level five times leverage can behave like sixteen. It then hands you a max safe size, an exit queue rank, and a clear verdict instead of a number you have to interpret.

In-app Trade

When you are ready to act, the Trade page runs a quick honeypot and authority check, then lets you swap through Jupiter without leaving the dashboard. The point is to keep the risk read and the action on the same screen, so you are not tab hopping while a token moves.

Smart Money and Wallet X-Ray

The platform tracks the most profitable wallets on the network and ranks them by real results. Open any wallet in Wallet X-Ray to see its behavior pattern, its history, and what it is holding. When a top wallet opens a new position, that shows up in the feed.

Narrative Intelligence

Money on Solana rotates by theme. The Narrative Brief, Pulse, and X-Ray views track which sectors are heating up, AI agents, memes, DePIN, and where capital is flowing, using news velocity and on-chain activity together.

06 | How a Signal Is Born

Every signal in the feed follows the same short path. There is no black box rating you have to take on faith. Each alert carries the token, the reason, and the data that triggered it, so you can judge it for yourself.

How a signal is born
1. DETECT
A new launch, a whale buy, an LP pull, or a smart wallet entry hits the pipeline.
2. SCORE
The relevant engine grades it 0 to 100 and notes which data sources fired.
3. CLASSIFY
The score plus context becomes an ENTRY, EXIT, or WATCH call with a confidence level.
4. SURFACE
It lands in the live feed with the token, the reason, and the on-chain evidence attached.

A signal is context, not a command. Some are clear calls to act. Others just tell you something is happening and worth a look. The confidence level and the sources are shown so you can weigh it.

07 | Technology and Architecture

The stack is deliberately boring where it should be and fast where it matters. A React front end talks to an Express back end that holds live WebSocket connections, scores incoming data, and writes to a PostgreSQL database through Prisma. External data comes from a short list of well-known providers.

Technology stack
Client
React 19, Vite, Tailwind, Recharts and D3 for charts
API and real time
Express, WebSocket streams, scheduled refresh jobs
Data
Prisma with PostgreSQL, in-memory caches for hot reads
Integrations
Helius, DexScreener, RugCheck, Jupiter, Bubblemaps, CoinGecko, GDELT, Telegram

Hot reads are cached so the dashboard stays responsive, and refresh jobs run on a schedule tuned to each source, frequent for on-chain data and lighter for news.
